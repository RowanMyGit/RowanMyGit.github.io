this is the aweful shoofly pie

My dad has had this recipe in the family for many years. apperently it is Amish. In total I think In the process of eating it we wasted about 3 pounds of uneaten pie.

This is the ingrediant list:

  • 1 cup molassses
  • 3/4 cup hot water
  • 1 egg
  • pie crust 9"
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup shortening

Ordered steps:(guesing)

  1. mix baking soda molasis water and the egg in a bowl
  2. mix the remaining ingrediants (besides pie crust) in another bowl
  3. prepare pie crust in tin
  4. pre heat oven 400°
  5. pour wet mix into crust
  6. lay dry mix on wet mix
  7. let dry mix sink
  8. decrease oven temp by 50°
  9. cook in oven for 15 mins
  10. let rest till toothpick test works
