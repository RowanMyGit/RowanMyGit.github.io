These are Ms.Sharttle's lemon squares

The story behind Ms.Sharttle's lemon squares is quite amusing. My father, who lived in pensilvania, attended a bake sale at a fire station. His family bought a few lemon squares and absolutly loved them. My dad who was young at the time was aspireing to be a detective. So his first case that he wanted to take on was finding out who made those delicious lemon squares. So he called the fire station as a 7 - 10 year old. And managed to get a hold of the manager of the bake sale and he got the number of Ms. Sharttle. My grandmother then called Ms. Sharttle and aquired the recipe. Honestly I thought that this would be more ammusing in my head but it's chill.
