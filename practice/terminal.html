How to navigate and use Vim

Navigating vim

  • When in normal mode it is easy to navigate with the: H, J,K,L keys
  • It is easy to jump multiple lines while in normal mode by typing [the amount you want to skip] and the direction e.g. 5k, for going up by 5 lines.

    Useful tips and tricks when working in Vim

    • you can delete words at a time in insert mode with Command + w
